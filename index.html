Publication

Malaysian Journal of Chemistry · Vol 28 No 4 · 31 Aug 2026

Preliminary Evaluation of a Barley-Based Polyaspartic Acid Formulation for Calcium Carbonate Scale Control

Wan Muhamad Syahrizal Wan Zailani, Shaziera Omar, Mohd Zaidi Jaafar, Mohd Akhmal Muhamad Sidek, Asiah Nusaibah Masri, Amni Haslinda Alpandi

A green CaCO₃ scale inhibitor combining barley powder and polyaspartic acid (PASP) was developed to address flow assurance challenges and the toxicity concerns of conventional inhibitors. The 7 wt.% barley and 1 wt.% PASP formulation reached 42.6% inhibition efficiency. TQ GC–MS identified fatty acid derivatives and amides that may act through calcium-ion complexation and crystal growth modification.
